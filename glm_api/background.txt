## 1. SURGERY BACKGROUND
================================================================================

Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (LC) is a minimally invasive surgical procedure to 
remove the gallbladder. It is one of the most commonly performed abdominal surgeries.

Key Anatomical Structures:
- Gallbladder: Pear-shaped organ that stores bile
- Cystic Duct: Tubular structure connecting gallbladder to common bile duct
- Cystic Artery: Blood vessel supplying the gallbladder
- Hepatocystic Triangle (Calot's Triangle): Triangular anatomical space bounded by:
  - Cystic duct (inferior)
  - Common hepatic duct (medial)
  - Inferior surface of liver (superior)
- Cystic Plate: Connective tissue layer between gallbladder and liver bed

================================================================================
## 2. SURGICAL PHASES
================================================================================

The procedure consists of 7 main phases:

1. **Preparation**
   - Setup, trocar insertion, patient positioning
   - Initial visualization of surgical field

2. **Calot Triangle Dissection**
   - Critical phase for identifying key structures
   - Careful dissection to expose cystic duct and artery
   - Goal: Achieve Critical View of Safety (CVS)

3. **Clipping and Cutting**
   - Application of clips to cystic duct and artery
   - Division of these structures
   - Requires confirmed CVS before proceeding

4. **Gallbladder Dissection**
   - Separation of gallbladder from liver bed
   - Progressive detachment using electrocautery
   - Control of bleeding from cystic plate

5. **Gallbladder Retraction**
   - Manipulation and positioning of gallbladder
   - Provides optimal exposure during dissection

6. **Cleaning and Coagulation**
   - Hemostasis of bleeding points
   - Irrigation and aspiration
   - Final inspection of surgical field

7. **Gallbladder Packaging**
   - Placement in retrieval bag
   - Extraction through trocar site

================================================================================
## 3. CRITICAL VIEW OF SAFETY (CVS)
================================================================================

CVS is a technique to prevent bile duct injuries. ALL THREE criteria must be met:

**CVS Criteria (all must be YES for CVS = TRUE):**

1. **Two Tubular Structures**
   - Only two tubular structures (cystic duct and cystic artery) should connect 
     to the gallbladder
   - No additional structures should be visible entering the gallbladder

2. **Hepatocystic Triangle Cleared**
   - The hepatocystic triangle (Calot's triangle) must be cleared of fat and 
     fibrous tissue
   - The underlying liver should be visible through the cleared triangle
   - Provides clear visualization of the structures

3. **Lower Gallbladder Detached**
   - The lower third of the gallbladder must be detached from the liver bed
   - Confirms the structures being clipped are truly the cystic duct and artery
   - Not the common bile duct or hepatic duct

**CVS Status:**
- CVS = TRUE only when ALL THREE criteria are satisfied
- CVS = FALSE if ANY criterion is not met

## 4. TISSUE RECOGNITION
================================================================================

### Cystic Duct
- Location: Biliary system, connects gallbladder to common bile duct
- Appearance: Tubular, smooth, membranous
- Characteristics: Well-defined boundaries, minimal vasculature
- Color: Pale/white, semi-translucent

### Cystic Artery
- Location: Supplies blood to gallbladder, typically runs parallel to cystic duct
- Appearance: Slender, smooth, tubular
- Characteristics: Distinct boundaries, prominent pulsatile vasculature
- Color: Reddish due to blood content

### Cystic Plate
- Location: Gallbladder bed (interface between gallbladder and liver)
- Appearance: Flat, membranous, slightly granular
- Characteristics: Broad boundaries, less distinct edges
- Color: Brownish-red, similar to liver surface

### Calot's Triangle (Hepatocystic Triangle)
- Location: Biliary region, bounded by key structures
- Appearance: Triangular area with visible underlying structures when cleared
- Characteristics: Contains cystic artery, lymph nodes, and connective tissue
- When cleared: Liver surface visible, triangle appears semi-opaque

### Gallbladder
- Location: Attached to inferior surface of liver
- Appearance: Pear-shaped, smooth, shiny surface
- Characteristics: Well-defined boundaries, thin wall
- Color: Blue-green (bile content), may appear distended or contracted

## 5. TOOL RECOGNITION
================================================================================

### Laparoscopic Grasper
- Purpose: Grasping, retracting, and manipulating tissues
- Color: Metallic silver jaws/tips, metallic shaft
- Shape: Straight design with jaws, slight inward curve at tips
- Features: Fine serrations on jaws, central pivot joint, oval-shaped jaw openings

### Irrigation/Suction Tool (Irrigator)
- Purpose: Irrigation and suction of surgical field
- Color: Metallic silver throughout
- Shape: Cylindrical shaft with tapered, rounded tip
- Features: Three circular fenestrations near tip, no jaws or grasping mechanism

### Laparoscopic Clip Applier (Clipper)
- Purpose: Applying metal clips to cystic duct and artery
- Color: Metallic silver jaws, metallic main body
- Shape: Slightly curved profile with beveled tips
- Features: Clamping surface without teeth/serrations, pivot joint for control

### Laparoscopic Scissors
- Purpose: Cutting tissue after clip application
- Color: Metallic silver blades and body
- Shape: Straight, elongated with pointed tips
- Features: Two crossing blades, sharp beveled edges, pivot joint at midpoint

### Laparoscopic Hook (Electrocautery Hook)
- Purpose: Dissection using electrocautery
- Color: White/light insulated tip (may appear reddish from blood), metallic shaft
- Shape: Hook-shaped with 45-degree curved tip, tapering to fine point
- Features: Single-ended, cylindrical shaft, no jaws or fenestrations

### Bipolar Forceps
- Purpose: Hemostasis through bipolar coagulation
- Color: Metallic silver jaws, blue insulated section, metallic body
- Shape: Flat, elongated jaws with fine serrations
- Features: Oval-shaped jaw openings, gentle curved tips, central pivot joint

================================================================================
## 6. ACTION RECOGNITION
================================================================================

Common surgical actions during laparoscopic cholecystectomy:

### Dissection Actions
- **Hooking**: Using electrocautery hook to separate tissues
- **Cutting**: Dividing structures with scissors
- **Dissecting**: Separating tissue planes

### Manipulation Actions
- **Grasping**: Holding tissue with grasper
- **Retracting**: Pulling tissue to expose surgical field
- **Lifting**: Elevating gallbladder or tissue

### Hemostasis Actions
- **Coagulating**: Applying electrical current for hemostasis
- **Clipping**: Applying metal clips to vessels/ducts

### Irrigation Actions
- **Irrigating**: Flushing surgical field with saline
- **Suctioning**: Removing fluid/blood from field

### Assessment Actions
- **Inspecting**: Visual examination of structures
- **Identifying**: Confirming anatomical structures

## 7. TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY RULES
================================================================================

For frame-by-frame analysis, maintain temporal consistency:

1. **Phase Progression**: Phases generally progress forward (Preparation → 
   CalotTriangleDissection → ClippingCutting → etc.)
   - Phase regression is rare but can occur (e.g., returning to dissection 
     if complications arise)

2. **CVS Temporal Logic**:
   - CVS typically achieved during CalotTriangleDissection phase
   - Once CVS is TRUE, it remains TRUE unless view is obscured
   - ClippingCutting should only occur AFTER CVS is TRUE

3. **Tool Consistency**:
   - Tools don't suddenly appear/disappear between consecutive frames
   - Tool transitions should be gradual
   - Multiple tools can be present simultaneously

4. **Action Continuity**:
   - Actions typically span multiple frames
   - Abrupt action changes should be verified
   - Concurrent actions are possible (e.g., grasping + coagulating)

================================================================================
## 8. QUALITY INDICATORS
================================================================================

Signs of good surgical technique:
- Clear identification of CVS before clipping
- Minimal bleeding
- Precise tissue handling
- Appropriate use of electrocautery
- Clear visualization throughout

Warning signs:
- Excessive bleeding
- Unclear anatomy
- Proceeding without clear CVS
- Tissue damage from excessive cautery
- Poor visualization due to smoke/blood

- hem_loc: 可见的Hem-o-lok数量（蓝/紫/绿/白色聚合物锁定夹），无则填0

## 阶段逻辑约束

**严格顺序阶段**：
- 准备阶段 → 只在手术开始
- 夹闭切断阶段 → CVS确认后一次性完成
- 胆囊取出阶段 → 只在手术最后

**可交替出现**：
- 胆囊牵拉阶段、清洁凝血阶段、肝胆三角解剖阶段、胆囊分离阶段

**阶段转换规则表**（后处理强制约束）：
| 当前阶段 | 允许转换到的阶段 |
|---------|-----------------|
| 准备阶段 | 肝胆三角解剖、胆囊牵拉 |
| 肝胆三角解剖阶段 | 夹闭切断、胆囊牵拉、准备阶段 |
| 夹闭切断阶段 | 胆囊分离、肝胆三角解剖 |
| 胆囊分离阶段 | 清洁凝血、胆囊牵拉、夹闭切断 |
| 胆囊牵拉阶段 | 肝胆三角解剖、胆囊分离、清洁凝血 |
| 清洁凝血阶段 | 胆囊取出、胆囊分离 |
| 胆囊取出阶段 | **仅允许清洁凝血**（禁止所有其他阶段！）|

**判断规则**：
1. 如果历史已出现非准备阶段，当前禁止回退到准备阶段
2. 如果历史已**连续2个窗口**判断为胆囊取出阶段，后续禁止回退到：
   - 胆囊分离、胆囊牵拉、夹闭切断、肝胆三角解剖、准备阶段
   - 仅允许清洁凝血阶段（术后止血清理）

**注意**：连续确认机制可避免单个窗口误判导致的约束误触发
